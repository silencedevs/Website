## Legal review still required

The operator says the project is private, free and open source, with no sales. Operator jurisdiction, required name/address/contact and applicable hosting/privacy details remain unresolved. No private address has been requested for publication or inserted into the site. No site was deployed.

For a German operator, free/hobby status does not by itself establish the exclusively personal/family exception. A public script community requires case-specific assessment. A purely digital mailbox, postbox or parcel locker should not be represented as a valid service address. No paid address provider was selected or endorsed.

- [Media authority NRW: transparency and imprint](https://www.medienanstalt-nrw.de/aufsicht/transparenz-im-internet.html), including §18 MStV and the insufficient postbox.
- [§5 DDG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html), commercial service disclosure scope.
- [Media State Treaty](https://www.die-medienanstalten.de/service/rechtsgrundlagen/medienstaatsvertrag).
- [State privacy authority: cookies and consent](https://www.datenschutz.rlp.de/themen/cookies-und-einwilligungsbanner). This implementation sets no cookies, reads no storage and embeds no third parties.

Legal draft pages and the launch check identify missing facts. `legalReviewed` and `imageRightsConfirmed` are false. The Pages deploy job intentionally stops while these material prerequisites are incomplete. This is a configuration gate, not legal certification.
